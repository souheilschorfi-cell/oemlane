Write to suppliers in their language

Messages are written the way Chinese export sales staff write, from the first RFQ to a firm escalation. Fill the highlighted placeholders once in each language. This is the start of message M01.

EnglishRFQ – [Product type] for [Vehicle model] – [Your company], [Your country]
Dear [Contact name],

My name is [Your name]. I am the purchasing manager of [Your company], an auto parts importer and distributor in [Your country].

We found your company on [Source] and would like to receive your best quotation for the attached list. It shows each OE number, the vehicle application and the estimated quantity per order.

Please quote for each item:
- Unit price in USD, [Incoterm] [Port of loading] (Incoterms 2020)
- MOQ per OE number
- Production lead time after deposit
- Payment terms

Questions

Is this a list of suppliers?

No. It is a method and a set of tools. The guide shows where parts are made in China and how to find and check suppliers yourself, and the tools keep your own supplier records.

Who is it for?

Importers, distributors, wholesalers, workshops and online parts sellers who buy, or are about to buy, auto parts from China, from a first mixed container to monthly orders.

Which software do I need?

Microsoft Excel (Windows or Mac) or LibreOffice for the Excel tools, and any PDF reader. Google Sheets can import the files; check the formulas there, and note that sheet protection is not kept.

Is the Chinese written by a machine?

No. The messages are written in the register Chinese export sales staff use, in simplified Chinese, and each one has a short WeChat version.

Does it give duty rates for my country?

No. Rates depend on your country and on the HS code of each part. The landed cost calculator lets you enter them, and the guide explains what to ask your customs broker.

What is the difference between Standard and Pro?

Standard has everything you need to run a sourcing project, with protected formulas. Pro unlocks every file so you can edit it and add your logo, adds Word versions, all 60 messages and all 35 part families, and includes every future update.

Can I share it with my team?

Yes, inside your own business. Please do not share or resell the files outside it.

What if it is not what I expected?

Write to us within 14 days of purchase and we will refund you.
