Where China's auto parts are made: 1,832 AAG 2026 exhibitors by province

Updated 29/10/2026. Adapted from chapter material of the OEMLane sourcing guide.

China's parts industry is concentrated in clusters: towns where hundreds of companies make similar products and share material suppliers, skilled workers and markets. Knowing them helps you search in the right place, plan visits and judge whether a supplier sits close to its own supply chain.

What one fair's exhibitor list shows

AAG 2026, Auto Aftermarket Guangzhou, was held from 19/08/2026 to 21/08/2026. Its exhibitor list dated 31/07/2026 counts 1,832 exhibitors, and we read each company's province and city from its registered Chinese name.

Three limits: these companies chose an aftermarket export fair, so they are not the whole industry; a name shows where a company is registered, not always where it produces; and traders and factories are counted together.

Clusters and what they are known for

These specialisations are documented in Chinese industry and government sources. "Known for" does not mean "the only place".

Cluster Province Known for
Wenzhou area, including Ruian Zhejiang Auto electrical parts (the best-known line), instruments, small motors, switches, filters, pumps, locks
Taizhou area, including Yuhuan Zhejiang Auto and motorcycle parts; Yuhuan's production centres on engine and chassis systems
Qinghe Hebei Control cables, seals and filters, strong in commercial-vehicle parts
Danyang Jiangsu Lamps, interior and exterior trim, electromechanical parts, moulds
Guangzhou Guangdong A carmaking centre with large wholesale parts markets, where many traders offer wide ranges and mixed orders

How to use cluster knowledge

  • Search with the place name in Chinese: 瑞安 汽车电器 (Ruian auto electrical), 清河 密封件 (Qinghe seals), 丹阳 车灯 (Danyang lamps).
  • Ask where the goods are made. A Guangzhou trader selling seals probably buys them in another province: normal, but you want to know.
  • Check the logic of the offer. A small company offering both pistons and headlamps is probably a trader.
  • Think about the port. Zhejiang usually ships from Ningbo or Shanghai, Guangdong from Nansha or Shenzhen, Hebei and Shandong from Tianjin or Qingdao.

In short

  • Parts come from clusters: Zhejiang alone has 40.17% of AAG 2026 exhibitors, and four provinces have 81.60%.
  • The Wenzhou area is the largest concentration in that list, known above all for auto electrical parts.
  • Qinghe is known for cables, seals and filters, Danyang for lamps and trim, Yuhuan for engine and chassis parts.
